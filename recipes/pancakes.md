# Pancakes

## Ingredients
- 200 g flour
- 2 eggs
- 300 ml milk
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- A pinch of salt

## Instructions
1. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt.
2. Add the eggs and milk.
3. Mix until the batter is smooth.
4. Heat a frying pan.
5. Pour a small amount of batter into the pan.
6. Cook each side until golden brown.
7. Serve warm.
